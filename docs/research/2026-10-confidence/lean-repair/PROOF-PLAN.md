# Independent prospective proof plan

Frozen design before Opus revision development. Designer: native Codex task `/root/lean_repair_proof_design`, separate from implementer. No model calls or production protocol edits by designer.

## Revision acceptance

1. Preserve intent-first acceptance: required behaviors, important regressions, consequential unknowns, constraints and actual behavior boundaries remain explicit. Intake is ready when no unresolved REQUIRED consequential choice or necessary fact blocks the next authorized action. Optional questions may be deferred visibly without blocking that action. A missing necessary fact stays a required gap, never an exclusion merely to finish intake; no proof that future discovery can never matter is required.
2. Keep required coverage visible at completion: reread accepted scope; pair each required behavior with current executed evidence or a resolvable inspected artifact. Distinguish requirement coverage, execution success and source freshness. Unpaired/failed/unavailable proof remains required remaining work.
3. State lean routing once: Opus owns substantial development and its own bounded check/repair; Haiku handles bounded extraction/discovery only when cheaper to check; Codex checks independently and may repair observed concrete defects. Ordinary direct work and unavailable-route fallbacks continue authorized scope.
4. Independent Codex repair must retain what it checked, the concrete finding, changed paths, and rerun affected proof. When reviewer repairs, distinguish its check/repair from independent post-repair verification; do not represent its own passing repair check as another independent review. Required independent review remains required under the existing triggers.
5. Preserve proportionality and autonomy without introducing obligatory ceremony, new framework/runtime, universal exhaustive testing, invented model availability, or extra approval gates. No new command-line enforcement is required for instruction-only lean revision.

## Revision checks

- Inspect exact changed instruction/reference text against obligations 1–5 and original accepted wording. Trace linked guidance so contradictory old role ownership or stopping conditions do not survive.
- Focused existing package validation/unit tests plus diff hygiene where affected. No duplicated test merely asserting copied instruction strings.
- Independent adversarial review asks whether a plausible agent can mark a requirement optional/covered without evidence; stop intake at a convenient token cutoff; silently reuse stale proof; or label a repairer as independent post-repair reviewer. Treat missing relevant role/evidence as a gap.
- Fresh comparison is small feasibility evidence, not a general quality claim. Freeze historical task IDs, prompts, requirements, production scope, baseline source and grader before attempts. Report raw pre-repair and post-repair outcomes separately, with repair actor and paths; arm budgets and packet boundaries must be the same.
